2. Description
     The Medtronic Launcher Guide Catheter is constructed with an inner liner, stainless steel
     braid, outer shaft jacket, sleeve, marker band and a soft distal tip. The inner lumen of the
     Launcher Guide Catheter has a thin lubricious coating.
