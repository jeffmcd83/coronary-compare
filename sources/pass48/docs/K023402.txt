2. Description
   The Medtronic Launcher Guide Catheter is constructed with an inner liner, stainless steel
   braid, outer shaft jacket, sleeve, marker band and a soft distal tip. The inner lumen of the
   Launcher Guide Catheter has a thin lubricious coating.


3. Intended Use
   The Medtronic Launcher Guide Catheter is designed to provide a pathway through which
   therapeutic devices are introduced. The guiding catheter is intended for used in the coronary
   or peripheral vascular system.


4. Technological Characteristics
    The 8F Launcher Guide Catheters are manufactured similar to the legally marketed predicate
    7F Guide Catheters.

    The manufacturing processes, materials and design are substantially equivalent to the 7F
    Launcher Guide Catheter.
