NHanceIT

POWER THROUGH COMPLEXITY WITH PRECISION & CONTROL

The IMDS NHanceIT Guide Wire Support Catheter, featuring a hydrophilic coating, is a guidewire exchange and infusion device designed for vascular use, supporting guidewire access, exchange, and the delivery of diagnostic agents.

When forward progress stalls in the most resistant lesion, NHanceIT delivers best-in-class penetration power, true 1:1 torque response, and a metal-reinforced tip to support reliable lesion entry. It maintains superior force transfer from hub to tip, avoiding the force loss seen with conventional single lumen microcatheters, while its metal-reinforced tip ensures integrity for reliable entry in highly calcified, tortuous anatomy. With a true radiopaque tip for clear visualization and precise catheter rotation, NHanceIT is engineered to work seamlessly with TrapIT, enabling true 6F OTW microcatheter exchange.
