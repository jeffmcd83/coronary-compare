Runthrough™ NS PTCA Guide Wire |​ The Guide Wire Family Designed for Everyday and Complex Coronary Interventions ​
2
neutrals-100

The Runthrough™ NS PTCA Guide Wire family is engineered to support a variety of coronary procedures, from workhorse to challenging interventions.​

The portfolio offers tip loads ranging from 0.6 gf to 3.3 gf, providing varying levels of support, different distal coating options, and is designed to meet the needs of both routine and more complex cases1.​
