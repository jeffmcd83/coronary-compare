   4. Device Description:
      The Small diameter core wire is a guide wire used in various percutaneous medical procedures
      to facilitate the placement of catheters and other interventional devices within the body. Its
      primary function is to provide a stable and precise pathway for the introduction of these medical
      tools. The Small diameter core wire is used to position catheters, sheaths, or other medical
      devices precisely within the body, ensuring that they are in the correct location for the intended
      treatment. The radiopacity helps in the visualization of the guidewire through a medical imaging
      system to track their position and guide the procedure. The operational principle and mechanism
      of action of the subject device is same as that of the legally marketed predicate device, NitrexTM
      Nitinol Guidewire (K024021).

       The Small diameter core is wire made up of a core wire and a radiopaque coil at the distal end.
       The core wire is placed inside a protective hoop. The device is available in two models: CMAN-
       000-14 with a core wire diameter of 0.014` and the CMAN-000-00 with a core wire diameter of
       0. 018`.The core wire in each model is available in three different lengths: 40, 50, and 80 cm.
       The distal end of the coil part is straight and made soft to easily bend with the vessel curve. In the
       package the guide wire is covered by a protective hoop made of HDEP.

       The subject device is a sterile product, labeled as sterile, single use only, and non-pyrogenic. It
       is packed in the Tyvek pouch and sterilized with Ethelyene oxide. Hydrophilic and/
       or hydrophobic lubricious coatings are not used in the subject device. The Small diameter
       core wire is intended to be used by healthcare professionals in hospitals. The intended use,
       design, scientific technology, and performance of the subject device are comparable to the
       predicate device.



   5. Indication for use statement:
