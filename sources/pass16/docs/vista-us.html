VISTA BRITE TIP Guiding Catheter

A complete system of large lumen guiding catheters that are optimized to answer clinical needs. The performance-based design of each catheter easily meets strength, control and delivery requirements in a wide range of anatomies.

Product Description

Designed to maintain shape in lengthy cases and challenging procedures, the Cordis VISTA BRITE TIP® Guiding Catheters are indicated for the delivery of interventional devices to coronary or peripheral vascular systems.

  • Delivers support, torque control and lumen integrity with proprietary hybrid braid construction.
  • Promotes coaxial alignment and kink resistance with a multi-segment design, which helps balances strength and flexibility.
  • Eases device delivery with a PTFE lining and a consistent diameter from hub to tip.
  • Facilitates atraumatic engagement by providing excellent visibility with a soft, radiopaque tip.
