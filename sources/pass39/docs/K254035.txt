 Device Description Summary                                                                               21 CFR 807.92(a)(4)
The Mayumi Thrombectomy System is a single-use, sterile, non-pyrogenic medical device designed for use in peripheral vasculature
and available in two configurations – the 20Fr and the 24Fr systems based on the Aspiration Catheter outer diameter (OD).
The Mayumi system consists of the following components, packaged together:
 Mayumi Aspiration Catheter (20Fr or 24Fr)

 Dilator
 Y-Connector



 Intended Use/Indications for Use                                                                         21 CFR 807.92(a)(5)
The Mayumi Thrombectomy System is indicated for:

•The non-surgical removal of emboli and thrombi from blood vessels
•Injection, infusion and/or aspiration of contrast media and other fluids into or from a blood vessel

The Mayumi Thrombectomy System is intended for use in the peripheral vasculature.

Technological Comparison                                                                                       21 CFR 807.92(a)(6)
-Both the subject and predicate devices have same design for catheter sizes (20F and 24F), proximal and distal metal structures made of
stainless steel, proximal and distal jacket material made of PEBAX, straight tip configuration and a 0.035" guidewire compatibility. Both
devices use a 60cc Locking Syringe made of polycarbonate as the aspiration source.

-Both the subject and predicate devices have same principles of operations defined as a single-use, over-the-wire catheter-based system
for the minimally invasive treatment of thromboemboli in the peripheral vasculature.

-Both the subject and predicate devices have a similar mode of action. The catheter along with the provided dilator are inserted and
advanced to the thrombus over a pre-placed 0.035" guidewire. After removal of its dilator, thrombus is removed by aspiration with the
provided 60cc Locking syringe. After the procedure is complete, the aspiration catheter is removed from the patient.

-It is important to note that technological differences for mode of action between the devices is that FlowTriever Catheters can be used
for mechanical thrombectomy by deploying directly through the Triever20, or Triever24 catheters.

The Triever Catheters can also be used as a stand-alone device for vacuum assisted aspiration, which is the primary mode of action for
the subject device, Mayumi and for which the substantial equivalence is claimed. A detailed comparison to support substantial
equivalence to the predicate is provided herein:

   The Mayumi Thrombectomy System was subjected to GLP in-vivo animal testing that confirmed the device did not raise questions
   related to safe use.

   No clinical studies were performed. The subject device, Mayumi Thrombectomy System performed equivalent to the comparator
   device InariTriever24 and Triever20 catheters, in terms of device trackability, aspiration performance, etc. The passing results
   from the acute animal studies and performance bench testing are considered sufficient to support the intended use of the
   Mayumi Thrombectomy System. Minor technological differences between the subject and predicate devices do not raise
   different questions of safety, performance and effectiveness. The subject and predicate devices are therefore considered to
   be substantially equivalent for the claimed intended use.

   MicroVention (d/b/a Terumo Neuro) concludes through a review of the benchtop and non-clinical animal assessments,
   comparison of the device classification, indications for use, operating principle, technological characteristics and biocompatibility
   that the Mayumi™ Thrombectomy System is substantially equivalent to the predicate Inari Triever24 and Triver20 catheters
   cleared under K213402. Any differences between the subject device and the predicate device do not raise different questions of
   safety and effectiveness.
