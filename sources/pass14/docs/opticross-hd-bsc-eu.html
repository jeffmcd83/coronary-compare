Product Overview

OPTICROSS™ HD

Coronary Imaging Catheter

High-Resolution Imaging

A 60 MHz wideband transducer increases lateral and axial resolution for superior image quality to visualize all three vessel layers, plaque morphology, and improves stent strut visibility for better optimization.

Proven Deliverability

Available in 5F and 6F, catheters feature a trusted, well-balanced design to ensure best-in-class deliverability.

Proven Deliverability

Available in 5F and 6F, catheters feature a trusted, well-balanced design to ensure best-in-class deliverability.

Unmatched Versatility

Expect exceptional near-field visibility for small vessel imaging as well as penetration depth of 6mm for large vessel assessment.
