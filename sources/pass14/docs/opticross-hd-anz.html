OPTICROSS™ HD IVUS Catheter Preparation Videos

  • Device Support for Healthcare Professionals
    • OPTICROSS™ HD IVUS Preparation & Set‑Up Videos

Clear IVUS imaging starts with consistent and appropriate catheter preparation. We have developed a step‑by‑step OPTICROSS™ catheter preparation video to support you and your team in becoming familiar with the process and applying it consistently in clinical practice.

Access the step‑by‑step videos below, along with downloadable PDF instructions, to support correct preparation and help achieve clear, reliable IVUS images.

OPTICROSS™ HD Motor Drive Unit (MDU) Set-Up Video

The video provides a step-by-step guide on how to properly attach the Motor Drive Unit (MDU) to a permanent sled outside the sterile field. This process should be done before using the OPTICROSS™ HD catheter. The setup allows for the MDU and sled to remain connected for future operations.

OPTICROSS HD Catheter Set-Up Catheter Prep Technique with Permanent Sled Video 

This video provides a detailed step‑by‑step guidance on OPTICROSS™ HD system setup focusing on preparing the permanent sled, MDU, sterile bag, and catheter for use. It covers:

  1. Handling and aligning of the sterile bag by both sterile and non-sterile operators 
  2. Catheter flushing technique to purge air bubbles and ensure proper catheter functionality.

IVUS Set-Up and Workflow: Imaging Catheters Preparation Instructions

This PDF guide provides step‑by‑step instructions for the preparation and setup of the OPTICROSS™ HD IVUS system.
