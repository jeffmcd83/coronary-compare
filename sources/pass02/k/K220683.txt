Indications for Use (Describe)

The INDIGO CAT RX Aspiration Catheters and INDIGO Separator 4
As part of the INDIGO Aspiration System, the INDIGO CAT RX Aspiration Catheters and INDIGO Separator 4 are
indicated for the removal of fresh, soft emboli and thrombi from vessels in the coronary and peripheral vasculature.

The INDIGO Aspiration Tubing
As part of the INDIGO Aspiration System, the INDIGO Sterile Aspiration Tubing is indicated to connect the INDIGO
CAT RX Aspiration Catheters to the Penumbra Aspiration Pump.

Penumbra Aspiration Pump
The Penumbra Aspiration Pump is indicated as a vacuum source for Penumbra Aspiration Systems.
